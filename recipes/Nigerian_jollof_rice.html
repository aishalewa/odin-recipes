Nigerian Jollof rice

Description

Nigerian jollof rice is a delicious and popular West African rice dish cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce. It is flavorful, aromatic, and commonly served at parties, celebrations, and family meals.

This Nigerian jollof rice recipe is made with long-grain parboiled rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, spices, and other seasonings. The rice is cooked in the seasoned tomato mixture until it is tender and full of flavor.

Ingredients

  • Long-grain parboiled rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Onion
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • Chicken stock
  • Thyme
  • Curry powder
  • Bay leaves
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes

Steps

  1. Wash and parboil the rice, then set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onions until smooth.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil and sauté some chopped onions.
  4. Add the tomato paste and fry for a few minutes.
  5. Add the blended tomato and pepper mixture and cook until the sauce thickens.
  6. Add the chicken stock, thyme, curry powder, bay leaves, seasoning cubes, and salt.
  7. Add the parboiled rice and mix well with the sauce.
  8. Cover the pot and allow the rice to cook on low heat until tender.
  9. Fluff the rice gently and serve hot.
