Nigerian Salad

Description

Nigerian salad is a colorful and refreshing side dish that is commonly served at Nigerian parties and celebrations. It is made with a mixture of fresh vegetables, pasta, sweetcorn and baked beans.

This Nigerian party salad combines crunchy vegetables with pasta, sweetcorn and baked beans to create a delicious and filling salad that can be served alongside a variety of meals.

Ingredients

  • 1 head of cabbage
  • 1 head of lettuce
  • 1 cucumber
  • 300g farfalle or fusili pasta (optional)
  • 5 carrots
  • 2 big tins of sweetcorn
  • 15 baby/salad tomatoes or 6 medium tomatoes
  • 1.5 tins of big baked beans

Steps

  1. Wash all the vegetables thoroughly.
  2. Shred the cabbage and lettuce into small pieces.
  3. Peel and grate or chop the carrots.
  4. Wash and slice the cucumber and tomatoes.
  5. Cook the pasta according to the instructions on the package, then drain and allow it to cool.
  6. Drain the sweetcorn and baked beans.
  7. Add the cabbage, lettuce, carrots, cucumber, tomatoes, sweetcorn and baked beans into a large bowl.
  8. Add the cooked pasta and gently mix all the ingredients together.
  9. Add salad cream or mayonnaise according to your preference and mix well.
  10. Chill the salad before serving.
