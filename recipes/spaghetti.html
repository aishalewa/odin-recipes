Spaghetti

Description

Jollof spaghetti is a delicious pasta dish prepared using a method inspired by Nigerian jollof rice. It is cooked in a rich tomato and pepper sauce and can be combined with vegetables and protein for a more satisfying meal.

This recipe is easy to prepare and can be made with chicken, beef or shrimps. The spaghetti is combined with the tomato sauce, fried vegetables and protein to create a flavorful and filling meal.

Ingredients

  • 500g spaghetti
  • 150g boneless raw chicken, beef or shrimps
  • 2 cups chicken or beef stock
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • 3 red bell peppers
  • 1 yellow bell pepper
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • 2 carrots or 1 cup frozen carrots
  • 1/2 can plum tomatoes or 3 medium tomatoes
  • 2 Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Spring onions
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Bay leaves
  • Knorr chicken cubes
  • Black pepper
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti in boiling water until tender, then drain and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, onion and Scotch bonnet peppers until smooth.
  3. Heat some oil in a saucepan and sauté half of the chopped onions until translucent.
  4. Add the blended pepper mixture, curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, seasoning cubes and salt.
  5. Allow the pepper mixture to fry until the oil rises to the top.
  6. In a separate frying pan or wok, heat some oil and sauté the remaining onions.
  7. Add the chopped garlic and cook until fragrant.
  8. Add the marinated chicken, beef or shrimps and fry until fully cooked.
  9. Add the chopped peppers, carrots and spring onions and sauté for 5–6 minutes.
  10. Set the fried vegetables and protein aside.
  11. Add the chicken or beef stock to the cooked pepper sauce and allow it to cook for 2–3 minutes.
  12. Add the cooked spaghetti and mix thoroughly with the sauce.
  13. Add the fried vegetables and protein and mix everything together.
  14. Allow the spaghetti to cook uncovered for another 2–3 minutes.
  15. Serve hot and enjoy.
